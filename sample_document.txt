CHAPTER 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY

1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
All students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject to be
eligible to appear in the end-semester examinations. Attendance is calculated
separately for lectures, tutorials, and laboratory sessions.

1.2 Attendance Tracking
Attendance is recorded digitally using the GIT Student Portal. Students must
mark their attendance within the first 15 minutes of each class session using
their registered student ID card. Proxy attendance is a serious disciplinary
offense and will result in suspension for a minimum of two weeks.

1.3 Medical Leave
Students who miss classes due to medical reasons must submit a medical
certificate from a registered medical practitioner within 7 working days of
returning to campus. Medical leave applications are reviewed by the Dean of
Student Affairs, Dr. Priya Sharma. Approved medical leave counts toward the
attendance requirement, up to a maximum of 15% of total classes.

1.4 Attendance Shortage
Students whose attendance falls below 75% will receive a warning notification
when their attendance reaches 78%. Students below 70% attendance will be
debarred from the end-semester examination and must repeat the course in the
next academic cycle. Appeals can be submitted to the Attendance Review
Committee before November 15, 2025.

CHAPTER 2: ASSIGNMENT SUBMISSION POLICY

2.1 Submission Format
All assignments must be submitted electronically through the GIT Learning
Management System (LMS) at https://lms.greenfield-institute.edu (fictional).
Submissions must be in PDF format unless the instructor specifies otherwise.
Each submission must include a cover page with the student's name, roll number,
subject code, and submission date.

2.2 Deadlines
Assignment deadlines are published on the LMS at the beginning of each
semester. The standard submission window closes at 11:59 PM IST on the due
date. Late submissions are accepted with the following penalties:

  - Up to 24 hours late: 10% marks deduction
  - 24 to 48 hours late: 25% marks deduction
  - 48 to 72 hours late: 50% marks deduction
  - More than 72 hours late: Assignment will not be accepted

2.3 Plagiarism Policy
All assignments are checked using automated plagiarism detection software.
A similarity score above 30% will be flagged for review. Confirmed plagiarism
results in zero marks for the assignment on the first offense, an F grade in
the course on the second offense, and academic probation on the third offense.

2.4 Group Assignments
Group assignments must clearly list all contributing members. Each group
member must submit an individual contribution statement. The maximum group
size is 4 students unless specified otherwise by the course instructor.

CHAPTER 3: LABORATORY SESSIONS

3.1 Laboratory Schedule
Laboratory sessions are held according to the timetable published by the
Department Office. Each lab session is 3 hours long. Students must arrive
within the first 10 minutes; late arrivals will not be permitted to enter
the laboratory.

3.2 Laboratory Requirements
Students must bring the following to every lab session:
  - Lab manual (printed or digital copy)
  - Lab record book (hardbound, 200 pages)
  - Student ID card
  - USB drive for saving work (minimum 8 GB)
  - Appropriate safety equipment as specified for the lab

3.3 Lab Report Submission
Lab reports must be submitted within one week of completing the experiment.
Reports must follow the standard format: Objective, Theory, Procedure,
Observations, Calculations, Results, and Conclusion. Handwritten lab records
are preferred for experimental courses. Digital submissions are accepted for
programming and simulation labs.

3.4 Safety Regulations
All students must complete the mandatory Laboratory Safety Training module
on the LMS before attending their first lab session. The training must be
completed by August 10, 2025. Students who have not completed the safety
training will not be allowed to enter any laboratory.

3.5 Equipment Damage
Students are financially responsible for any laboratory equipment damaged
due to negligence. The cost of damaged equipment will be added to the
student's fee account. All equipment damage must be reported immediately
to the Lab Supervisor, Mr. Rajesh Kumar.

CHAPTER 4: PROJECT DEADLINES AND GUIDELINES

4.1 Final Year Project Timeline
The final year project follows a two-semester schedule:

  Semester 7 (Odd Semester):
  - Topic Selection and Approval: August 25, 2025
  - Literature Survey Submission: September 15, 2025
  - Project Proposal Presentation: October 1, 2025
  - Mid-Semester Progress Review: October 20, 2025

  Semester 8 (Even Semester):
  - Implementation Phase 1 Review: February 1, 2026
  - Implementation Phase 2 Review: March 15, 2026
  - Final Project Report Submission: April 10, 2026
  - Project Demonstration and Viva: April 20-25, 2026

4.2 Project Report Format
The project report must be between 60 and 100 pages, formatted according to
the GIT Project Report Template available on the LMS. The report must include:
Abstract, Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Implementation,
Testing, Results, Conclusion, and References.

4.3 Project Guide
Each student or team must have an assigned faculty project guide. Students
must meet their project guide at least twice a month. Meeting logs must be
maintained and submitted with the final report.

4.4 External Projects
Students undertaking projects in collaboration with external organizations
must obtain prior approval from the Head of Department by August 15, 2025.
An MOU between the institute and the external organization is required.

CHAPTER 5: STUDENT SUPPORT SERVICES

5.1 Academic Counseling
Each student is assigned an academic counselor from the faculty. Counseling
sessions are held every Wednesday from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Students
struggling academically can request additional sessions through the
Student Affairs Office in Room 201, Block A.

5.2 Mental Health Support
The institute provides free counseling services through the Wellness Center,
located in Block C, Ground Floor. Appointments can be booked through the
student portal or by calling extension 4567. The Wellness Center is open
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. All sessions are strictly confidential.

5.3 Library Services
The Central Library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays and
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends. Students can borrow up to 5 books at a
time for a period of 14 days. The digital library provides access to over
50,000 e-books and 10,000 research journals through IEEE Xplore, Springer,
and ACM Digital Library subscriptions.

5.4 Financial Aid
Students facing financial difficulties can apply for the GIT Merit-cum-Means
Scholarship. Applications open on July 1, 2025, and close on August 31, 2025.
The scholarship covers up to 75% of tuition fees based on academic performance
and financial need. Contact the Financial Aid Office at finance@greenfield-
institute.edu (fictional).

5.5 Placement Cell
The Training and Placement Cell organizes campus recruitment drives from
September to March. Students must register on the placement portal by
August 20, 2025. The placement cell also conducts aptitude training,
mock interviews, and resume workshops starting from July 15, 2025.
